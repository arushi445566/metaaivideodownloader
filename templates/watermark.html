Why do Meta AI videos have a watermark?

Meta, like most generative-AI platforms, adds a small visible watermark to AI-generated videos so viewers can tell that the content was created by artificial intelligence. This is part of a broader industry move toward transparency and responsible AI labeling. The watermark is added to the version displayed inside the chat interface. Our tool helps you retrieve and save the cleanest available copy of a video you generated yourself, for personal archival and editing.

Is removing a Meta AI watermark allowed?

You should only remove watermarks from videos that you created yourself and that you have the right to use. Many creators want a clean copy of their own work for editing, portfolios, or re-uploading to platforms that re-encode video anyway. However, watermarks and AI-disclosure labels exist for a reason: if you publish AI-generated content publicly, consider disclosing that it was made with AI, and always follow Meta's AI Studio policies and your local laws. Do not remove watermarks to misrepresent ownership or to pass AI content off as something it is not. For more, read our guide to AI video copyright.
